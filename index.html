How it works

Three steps, one connected school day.

1

Get your pass

Android: install the Qynect app. iPhone or iPad: open the web pass in Safari. No install needed.

2

Register your pass

Enter your school ID, full name, and section. Qynect creates your personal QR pass automatically.

3

Scan at school

Show the pass to the scanner. Attendance is saved and linked parents are notified.

iPhone & iPad

No App Store download needed

Open the Qynect Web Pass in Safari, register once, and add it to your Home Screen. It creates the same live QR pass as the Android app.
